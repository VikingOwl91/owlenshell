## Context and original goals

The bake-off compares Slint, egui/wgpu, GTK4, Qt6/QML and Iced as possible frontends for a Rust-native modular Wayland shell. The original goals include shell correctness, idle behavior, startup and resource costs, integration with the existing SCTK platform, module/output lifecycle, maintainability and long-term extensibility. A rewrite is not assumed; existing domain, placement, configuration, persistence and coordinator concepts should be retained where practical. Historical Owlry measurements are background, not an OwlenShell benchmark baseline. See the [architecture audit](../bakeoffs/renderer/SOURCE-REFERENCES.md#source-ae5ff82ea0a7f64c), [comparison](../bakeoffs/renderer/SOURCE-REFERENCES.md#source-d4a7fc96adaca83e) and [shared protocol](../bakeoffs/renderer/SOURCE-REFERENCES.md#source-3971f4d3ce98f43f).

## Evidence and unresolved work

Five prototypes and isolated nested-compositor observations exist. Historical compositor captures, bounded 66-point color samples, egui local typed-object observations, isolated candidate-free two-background controls and targeted GTK/Iced source/build work are **PARTIAL** for their stated scopes. They do not establish equivalent complete scenes, actual alpha-buffer correctness, full-window static state or connection-scoped server lifecycle. No matched CPU/GPU/memory/startup comparison has been authorized or performed. The [readiness matrix](../bakeoffs/renderer/SOURCE-REFERENCES.md#source-2140e71b8be7ba02) records all five as BLOCKED.

The approved [Gate R/P protocol](../bakeoffs/renderer/GATE-PROPOSAL.md) requires scoped connection/surface configure, acknowledgement and submission association, equivalent scene and behavior, measurement validity and independent review. Gate P retains complete E1–E11 and production integration acceptance. The current implementation plan adds a private Niri producer, full-image analysis, and alpha-buffer evidence, all demanded by subsequent investigation tasks. **The Gate R protocol itself does not prescribe those as the only possible implementations.** Alternatives could be evaluated, but none may be silently substituted for approved evidence requirements. The current NO-GO remains unchanged.

Unresolved infrastructure includes a real connection-lifetime producer and negative controls; complete visual reference, text and edge methods; controlled candidate sessions; sustained state receipts; actual font/layout identification; a safe capture supervisor; and measurement attribution. Reviewed findings include a possible startup/cleanup race in the still-disabled static driver, and a text-distance criterion that can miss a thin absent stroke. See [focused review](../bakeoffs/renderer/SOURCE-REFERENCES.md#source-d2a49822d62345b9) and [visual proposal](../bakeoffs/renderer/SOURCE-REFERENCES.md#source-958da9b78383a4c3).

## Proportionality and effort

Connection-scoped and equivalent-workload evidence can prevent wrong conclusions about a toolkit, but building a compositor diagnostic and analyzer may cost substantially more than an initial research question warrants. Conversely, cheaper measurements can be dominated by differences in platform integration, fonts, work performed, GPU drivers and unrelated background load. No exact completion estimate has been validated. The durations below are **rough planning ranges, not measured effort or commitments**, and exclude unexpected dependency, review and environment problems. None changes the gates or authorizes execution.

| Option | Potentially useful evidence | Principal risks and unsupported claims | Illustrative effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complete approved Gate R | Reviewed comparisons for the exact candidate set, workload, metrics and environment. | Highest instrumentation cost; still not Gate P, physical hotplug, energy or input-to-photon evidence. | Several engineer-weeks; high uncertainty. |
| Separately authorized single-candidate exploratory diagnostics | Detect gross process resource growth, unexpected wakeups or setup failures and form testable hypotheses. | Cannot establish cross-renderer superiority or workload equivalence; system effects and timing can mislead. | 1–3 engineer-days after a safe harness, plus review; highly uncertain. |
| Separately authorized approximate-scene comparisons | Reveal questions about broad resource envelopes under explicit scene differences. | Visual/runtime differences and shared GPU activity can dominate; adjacent numbers can be mistaken for a ranking. | 3–7 engineer-days plus review; high uncertainty. |
| Separately authorized offscreen or renderer-only micro-experiments | Examine narrowly specified rendering/model operations. | Does not represent layer-shell, full event loop, persistent idle or deployment cost. | 2–5 engineer-days per question; high uncertainty. |
| Architecture and maintenance investigation without performance measurement | Map Wayland ownership, Rust bridge complexity, dependency footprint and migration seams. | Interpretations are not observed developer productivity or proof of performance. | 1–3 engineer-days per bounded question; high uncertainty. |

## Accepted three-tier evidence framework

**Exploratory measurements — non-decision-grade:** each run would need a separate explicit authorization, scoped question, exact source/build/environment and instrumentation provenance, known confounders, failed trials, and a prominent permanent `EXPLORATORY — NON-DECISION-GRADE` label in a separate path/schema. Report the literal observation and uncertainty; avoid winners, weighted scores or statements of equivalence. Present confounders adjacent to results and prefer diagnostics aimed at a hypothesis over leaderboards. These observations could guide follow-up tests or highlight risks; they would not count as Gate R or Gate P evidence, establish relative superiority, or alone justify renderer selection. Any later use must cite the original classification, preserve negative/conflicting data and undergo a fresh explicit review against the then-approved criteria; never relabel old runs retroactively.

**Gate R benchmarks — already approved protocol:** research eligibility is separately evaluated for an exact candidate set, workload, metric and environment. Entry is evidence-based, not a performance threshold. Execution still requires explicit authorization. A Gate R result informs that scoped question; it is not production readiness.

**Gate P acceptance — already approved protocol:** full E1–E11 and integration requirements remain mandatory for production acceptance. Maintainers decide renderer selection and separately authorize integration.

## Resource and reproducibility considerations

A quota-limited `/tmp` can have far less usable capacity than `df` suggests. The [cleanup record](../bakeoffs/renderer/SOURCE-REFERENCES.md#source-4f25c8a3b21a2581) preserves exact reclaimed paths and measured quota change. A dedicated disk-backed isolated build directory is a possible alternative where explicitly writable and quota-verified; no global Cargo cache or production configuration needs to be modified. A provisional 5–10 GiB allocation is a planning envelope, **not a demonstrated Niri build requirement**. Determine the actual dependency closure, preserve the pristine lock and local overrides, check effective quota and system dependencies, and keep an abort reserve before a build. The [earlier preflight](../bakeoffs/renderer/SOURCE-REFERENCES.md#source-f59fac9abf662caa) records the missing cache entries and prior limit. After cleanup, a [private check and binary build](../bakeoffs/renderer/SOURCE-REFERENCES.md#source-82f2d46e27015fea) succeeded in this environment. An [incomplete diagnostic sketch](../bakeoffs/renderer/SOURCE-REFERENCES.md#source-750833e16d16f9d3) compiled but is not safe to enable and establishes no real lifecycle evidence.

Independent review of this proposal and cleanup evidence is [recorded here](../bakeoffs/renderer/SOURCE-REFERENCES.md#source-42a8c54bc2752d2c).

## Decision and questions for maintainers

**Decision:** Adopt the three-tier Exploratory / Gate R / Gate P evidence framework. Exploratory findings remain permanently non-decision-grade and must never be silently promoted into comparative benchmark evidence. Each exploratory measurement needs separate explicit authorization. Gate R and Gate P retain their previously approved requirements and historical NO-GOs. Questions for maintainers include which research questions warrant exploratory data, whether approximate cross-candidate tables are acceptable, and what evidence justifies the cost of completing Gate R. Renderer choice and integration remain separate maintainer decisions.

## Consequences and reconsideration

Keeping the existing gates avoids conflating measurement with acceptance but may delay comparable data. Introducing a distinct exploratory class could accelerate hypothesis formation while increasing the risk that weak data is treated as a result. Revisit this ADR when maintainers change the research questions, isolation or instrumentation becomes materially simpler, upstream toolkits change, or retained evidence exposes a misleading interpretation. Preserve all historical evidence and NO-GO decisions regardless of the choice.
