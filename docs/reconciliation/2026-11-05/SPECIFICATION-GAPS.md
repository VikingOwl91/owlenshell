## B0 review versus implementation readiness

No conflicting maintainer policy has been identified, and no new architectural choice is needed merely to review the K contracts. B0 is **not declared complete** by this package. Its contracts still require maintainer review; G14 and the B1 portions of G03/G04 must be sufficiently specified to close an implementation-ready B0 brief. G01 and the B1 observation/input portions of G02/G05 must be selected before live B1 validation is authorized.

Later gaps may remain explicitly assigned to B2–B5 without blocking review of B0's invariants. They cannot remain unresolved when their dependent interface or runtime acceptance is implemented. Single-writer and protected-schema policies also apply before any earlier shared-state write; placing their full implementation in B4 is not a waiver. Every B1–B6 implementation, runtime test and deployment still needs separate authorization.
