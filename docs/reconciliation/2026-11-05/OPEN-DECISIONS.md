## Approval sequence and ADR handling

Resolve D01–D04 enough to approve B0/B1; resolve lifecycle/scheduling details for B2, then output scope, config/persistence, and finally minimal services. D05–D10 do not need a full future product design before the first safe single-pair seam, but their affected interfaces must not be frozen prematurely. New state/composition, shutdown/recovery, configuration/persistence and session/IPC authority choices warrant focused ADRs when accepted. This pass creates none and edits no accepted ADR.

These choices require maintainer judgment about UX, reliability, compatibility and maintenance. No scores, weights or automatic renderer winner are proposed. The existing Slint decision remains the baseline; a later renderer reconsideration requires a new maintainer decision.
