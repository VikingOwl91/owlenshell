## Milestones and remaining maintainer choices

Unchanged dependency graph: **B0 → B1 → B2 → B3; B1+B2 → B4; B2+B4 → B5; applicable B3/B4/B5 evidence → B6**. G09/G11 protections apply early if shared/real state would be touched; timeline placement never waives them.

For formal B0 acceptance, maintainer review must accept or amend the concrete K refinements, especially G03/G04/G14 and the B1 minimum G05 action contract. This does not reopen approved D/P policies. For a later B1 runtime request, explicitly confirm the recommended environment (or approve another), complete its current version/build binding, and approve justified observation/cleanup bounds and the actual private execution plan. Product readiness semantics remain before B2; later storage/input/provider choices keep their recorded deadlines.

No new ADR is silently adopted. Retain proposed A01 (owner-thread concrete Slint boundary), A02 (canonical composition/lifetimes), A03 (ordered shutdown/lost connection), and A04–A07 only at their established prerequisites in [consolidation](CONSOLIDATION-AND-MILESTONES.md#proposed-adr-candidates--none-adopted). Generation/retirement refinements can inform A01/A02/A03; no accepted ownership/renderer ADR needs rewriting for these guards.

## Proposed formal B0 readiness recommendation

**GO for maintainer review and formal acceptance of the B0 specification package, subject to the independent-review verdict.** G03/G04/G14 now have concrete proposed answers and tests rather than policy-only placeholders. No contradiction requiring a new policy choice has been identified. **B0 is not declared accepted by this report.** An unconfirmed environment and execution bounds are explicit B1 runtime prerequisites, not fabricated completed work; they do not prevent acceptance of the architecture contracts themselves.

**NO-GO for B1 implementation/execution under the current documentation-only authorization.** A later request must separately authorize its exact implementation cut and runtime plan. Gate P remains unpassed; original S1–S6 scoped results, Niri multi-output BLOCKED, pointer-leave distinctions, historical NO-GOs, unexplored hardware/distributions and exploratory-only performance evidence all remain unchanged. No production, benchmark or publication action is implied.
